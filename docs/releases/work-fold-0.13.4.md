# work-fold 0.13.4

November 2, 2026

This release adds small animations as things open, close, and finish.

- Dialogs, menus, Settings pages, and new tabs arrive with short fades and movement. Closing tabs, folders, and toasts settle away while their neighbours close the gap.
- Sending a new Chat's first message moves its composer into place. Switching work-folders settles the left pane, and Worker activity dots mark a newly finished reply.
- Copy checkmarks, button presses, and the Latest button have small visual responses.
- Both Reduce Motion settings stop animations, including scripted effects already running. Folder-opening animations stay with the folder you opened and do not replay in another Files tree or during search.

Mac releases and automatic updates are available through the
[work-fold Mac release feed](https://github.com/Mat-Tom-Son/work-fold-mac-releases/releases).
