# work-fold 0.13.9

November 5, 2026

This release adds multiple selection in Files, a one-page Customize, and clearer buttons.

- Files selects several rows with ⌘-click and Shift-click. Attach them to a Chat, copy their paths, delete them, or drag them together.
- A refresh no longer closes folders you had open, and deleting a folder asks to confirm **Delete Folder**.
- The composer's context circle is its own button. It shows how full the Chat's context is and offers **Compact Chat**. Reasoning levels stay on one row.
- Customize work-folder is one page with Banner, Title, Icon, and Color sections. Title adds a background (Frosted, Solid, Accent, or None) and an icon style (None, Plain, Square, or Circle), and the previews match the header.
- The banner's title stays centered when the Files list scrolls.
- Buttons have a visible shape in light and dark and keep the normal pointer, like a Mac app. Labels no longer wrap at the smallest window size.

Mac releases and automatic updates are available through the
[work-fold Mac release feed](https://github.com/Mat-Tom-Son/work-fold-mac-releases/releases).
