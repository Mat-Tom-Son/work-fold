# work-fold 0.3.0

August 11, 2026

work-fold 0.3.0 ships the fold: one name for the management conversation, the
menu-bar popover, and "Your fold on the web," and one doctrine behind it — the
fold can do anything you can do in work-fold, with a receipt for everything,
while the few decisions that must stay yours come back to you as a click.

## The fold

- The management surface has one user-facing name. The popover is "Your
  fold"; Remote access appears as "Your fold on the web"; staged material
  arms the composer's **Fold it in** send action.
- `work-fold help` gains topics for every act family, and the fold's own
  instructions teach the complete verb surface and its etiquette.

## Every verb, receipted

- The act lane reaches human parity: chat lifecycle and compaction, History
  restore with automation-aware fencing, file move/rename/delete/create,
  content search, Library, Space rename/unregister and appearance, tool
  removal, and App Studio's authority-neutral lifecycle all execute as
  journaled, receipted acts with typed undo references.
- Receipts record the acting surface, decision lineage, and remote browser
  identity; version-1 journals remain readable and replay-safe.

## Decisions stay with the person

- Installing code, widening a standing power, and destroying irreversibly now
  stage inert needs-you decisions instead of executing: host-composed cards
  on the main window's flyout, the popover, and approved remote browsers,
  with expiry that never approves, denial that is recorded rather than
  retried, and cascade cancellation when a Space, app, or browser goes away.
- Standing policies — authored only in **Settings → The fold** — pre-approve
  narrow categories with an exercised-policy receipt each time; destruction,
  outward exposure, and routing enablement are never policy-eligible.
- A rootless file grant is approved with a folder picked at decision time in
  the main window's needs-you flyout.

## Routings

- Declared, deterministic cross-Space glue: a schedule or a settled Check or
  automation run triggers fixed Chat, files, and Check steps on the shared
  scheduler, with per-hop receipts, honest failure semantics, and Space
  removal suspending the routing rather than retargeting it. Enabling a
  routing is a needs-you decision; run-now, stop, disable, and delete are
  direct receipted verbs.

## The glance

- One computed digest — running work, what needs you, what changed since you
  last looked — on the main window, the popover, and your fold on the web,
  with per-surface seen markers and narration on demand.

## Pages your fold serves

- Publish one designated file as a live page at your `<name>.work-fold.com`
  address, or put a reviewed app Release at your address for viewers: served
  from your desktop through the relay, end-to-end encrypted with a key that
  lives only in the share link, rate- and byte-budgeted, and revocable in one
  act. An offline desktop is honestly asleep; an explicit, labeled snapshot
  opt-in can keep an encrypted copy at the bridge. Viewers are link-scoped
  and read-only, and hosted apps reach only a strictly viewer-safe broker
  subset, enforced on your computer.

## Settings → The fold

- One Settings section owns standing policies (create, edit, enable,
  disable, delete, under a fail-closed store attestation), publications
  (status, budgets, share-link reveal, snapshot control, revoke), and "Your
  fold on the web."
