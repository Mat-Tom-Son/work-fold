# work-fold 0.3.2

August 12, 2026

work-fold 0.3.2 gives every surface the finished folder-cube identity — one
mark, one lockup, one palette, and one type family across the desktop app,
the menu-bar fold, and your fold on the web.

## The folder-cube brand

- The app icon is the designer folder-cube: the warm top folder over the blue
  and green pair on the platform's rounded tile. Every size ships from the
  designer's own exports, including optically simplified small sizes, so the
  mark stays crisp from the Dock down to a 16px favicon.
- The menu bar uses the purpose-built template cube, recolored natively by
  macOS for light and dark menu bars.
- Lockups everywhere are the real horizontal lockup artwork — black on light
  themes, white on dark — in the popover header, onboarding, About, the
  loading screen, and the web headers.

## A navy-and-blue interface

- The terracotta-and-paper palette retires. Interface ink is deep navy, the
  interactive accent is bright blue, and light surfaces sit on the brand's
  light neutral field; dark mode moves to a navy slate with the bright blue
  accent.
- Filled controls keep readable contrast in both themes, and the native
  title bar, window backgrounds, and tooltips match the new palette.

## Brand typography

- Inter carries body text and controls; Poppins SemiBold carries interface
  headings and the landing page. Both fonts ship with the app and the web
  client — nothing loads from a CDN.
- Conversation and document content rendered from markdown keeps the body
  face for readability.

## Your fold on the web

- The landing page, sign-in, and the installable web app wear the same
  identity: new icons, theme colors, headers, and a proper link preview when
  the address is shared.
