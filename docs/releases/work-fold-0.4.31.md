# work-fold 0.4.31

September 12, 2026

Spaces are now called Folders, their assistants are Workers, and the management
assistant is the work-fold agent. Routings appear as Automations. Existing
folders, conversations, commands, and saved formats keep their identities.

Settings uses Appearance, Agents, General, Desktop, and About, without repeating
page titles above the controls. Rail hover tips no longer cover menus. New
and existing Automations can open an unsent draft with the work-fold agent;
running, enabling, and disabling still use the same recorded actions.

The menu-bar chat shows its conversation title, separate history and new-chat
actions, and a compact circular send button. History groups conversations into
Today, Yesterday, Earlier this week, Last week, and Older using local calendar
dates, including daylight-saving boundaries.

Web chat has a compact folder picker at the bottom of its sidebar, more space
around chat history, and connection status only when the desktop is unavailable.
The landing page and screenshots use the updated interface.

The text-review Check implementation fingerprint advances with its updated
model-setup message. Existing text-review Checks require re-enablement; their
files, criteria, and results are preserved. The Chrome extension protocol and
pending Store submission are unchanged.
