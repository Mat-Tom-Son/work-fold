# work-fold 0.4.45

October 9, 2026

This candidate brings the calmer desktop design into the application while preserving saved appearance and work-folder personalization.

- The distinctive folder banner stays central, with a clearer 112px identity band and matching light/dark customization previews.
- Controls share one styling family, with full-target hover feedback, stable pressed states and visible keyboard focus. Tabs have stronger separation and active states in both themes.
- Files and Chats share search fields, typography and row density. File tabs align the title, metadata, wrapping actions and readable document to one gutter.
- The composer focus ring encloses the whole input and its footer. Copy sits at the right edge beneath user messages, and hover reveals the timestamp to its left.
- History is a dated journal; restore review keeps conflicts, exclusions and coverage visible. Keyboard Shortcuts moves into Settings, including its keyboard and native-menu entry points.
- The desktop stylesheet replaces seven override sheets and removes unused styles for retired surfaces. Application palettes, fonts, reading settings, density, accessibility, presets and Undo remain authoritative; folder colors, icons, banners and framing retain their own identity.

Publication is tracked by the [Mac release feed](https://github.com/Mat-Tom-Son/work-fold-mac-releases/releases/latest); these candidate notes alone do not establish publication.
