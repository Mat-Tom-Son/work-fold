# work-fold 0.4.47

October 9, 2026

This corrects the remaining differences between the approved desktop demo and the shipped design.

- The work-folder banner and tabs now have equal 8px space above and below, and their top edges align. The shell keeps a 12px outer gutter beneath the native macOS title-bar strip.
- Rail buttons, menus, chat actions, model choices, and other controls use the demo's filled hover and stronger pressed states. Selected Files and Chats deepen their own work-folder tint; keyboard focus stays distinct and disabled controls stay quiet.
- Chat action menus regain an opaque background. AI Models and Shared Pages lose the old nested frames, matching the rest of Settings.
- Tabs retain their work-folder icons in narrow panes. The full composer focus boundary, right-aligned message Copy, matching Files/Chats searches and density, file-preview gutter, History journal, and Keyboard Shortcuts in Settings are retained.
- All five application palettes, custom accents, fonts, reading preferences, density, accessibility settings, and work-folder artwork remain authoritative. Rendered Chromium checks cover the actual stylesheet cascade in light and dark modes.

The [Mac release feed](https://github.com/Mat-Tom-Son/work-fold-mac-releases/releases/latest) records publication; source notes alone do not establish availability.
